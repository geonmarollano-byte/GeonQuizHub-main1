INFO & HOW TO PLAY

Normal Quiz

Pick a subject and quiz type (SUBJECT 1 / SUBJECT 2). Each path has 80 levels. You start with 8 lives and have 30 seconds per question. Correct answers earn score, coins and points; wrong answers cost a life. Reach Level 80 to win the path. Hitting 0 lives resets that path to Level 1.

Streaks

Consecutive correct answers raise your score multiplier up to ×3 and trigger milestone bonuses at 3, 5, 10, 15 and 20.

Other modes

Reviewer — practice by subject, questioner and topic filter with explanations. Daily Challenge — the same 10 questions for everyone each day; finish for +100 pts / +5 🪙. Story Quiz — read 10 stories and answer comprehension questions. Missions — 5 mission scenarios; +100 pts / +20 🪙 each, plus a bonus for all five.

Economy

Earn coins and points as you play. Convert points to coins (10 pts = 5 🪙). Spend coins in the Shop on Hint, 50/50, Time Boost, Life Token and Second Chance.

Saves

Your progress saves automatically in this browser. Use Menu → Export/Import to move saves between devices. Previous and New questioner progress are stored separately.
